## Market Segmentation

The DNA electrophoresis market can be segmented based on product, application, end-user, and region. In terms of product, the market includes gel electrophoresis systems, capillary electrophoresis systems, reagents, and consumables. Gel electrophoresis systems dominate the market due to their widespread usage in routine laboratory applications and cost-effectiveness. Capillary electrophoresis is expected to grow at a faster CAGR due to its high resolution, automation capability, and suitability for advanced research applications.

Application-wise, the market spans clinical diagnostics, research laboratories, forensic applications, academic institutions, and pharmaceutical research. Clinical diagnostics hold a significant market share, driven by the increasing use of electrophoresis in genetic testing and disease identification. Meanwhile, research laboratories and pharmaceutical sectors are increasingly adopting advanced electrophoresis techniques for protein characterization, drug development, and biomarker discovery.

Emerging economies such as China and India are witnessing rapid adoption of DNA electrophoresis technologies in both clinical and research domains.
